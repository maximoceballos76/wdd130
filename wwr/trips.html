Our Trips

Whether you want a relaxed float with the family or a heart-pounding run through the rapids, we have a river adventure for you. Every trip includes high-quality gear, a safety briefing and certified guides.

Featured Trips

Half-Day Adventure

Our most popular trip. Four hours of splashing rapids and calm stretches, ideal for first-timers and families who want a taste of whitewater.

  • Difficulty: Class III
  • Duration: 4 hours
  • Minimum age: 8
  • Price: $85 per person

Full-Day Expedition

Spend the whole day on the river. Paddle a long canyon, stop for a riverside lunch and finish with the biggest rapids of the day.

  • Difficulty: Class III-IV
  • Duration: 8 hours, lunch included
  • Minimum age: 12
  • Price: $150 per person

Rapids Rush

For thrill seekers. Three hours of fast, powerful rapids with a small crew and an experienced guide at the helm.

  • Difficulty: Class IV
  • Duration: 3 hours
  • Minimum age: 14
  • Price: $110 per person
